# Privacy Policy for Dreamkeepers

**Effective Date:** September 5, 2026
**Last Updated:** September 5, 2026

Oguzcan Budak ("we," "our," or "us") developed Dreamkeepers, a mobile collector role-playing game for iOS. This Privacy Policy explains what information Dreamkeepers collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding it.

By using Dreamkeepers, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

Dreamkeepers does **not** require you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, or any other directly-identifying information.

### Information Collected Automatically

- **Advertising identifier (IDFA):** If you grant permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency ("ATT") prompt, Dreamkeepers' advertising partner (Google Mobile Ads / AdMob) accesses your device's advertising identifier to personalize ads and measure ad performance. If you decline, Dreamkeepers still shows ads, but they are not personalized to you.
- **Ad interaction data:** Google Mobile Ads collects data such as ad impressions, clicks, and approximate (IP-based) location to serve and measure ads, per [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Game Center data:** If you sign in to Game Center, Apple's GameKit framework shares your Game Center player identifier, display name, and — where you use the in-app friends/leaderboard features — your Game Center friends' display names and count with the app, in order to show leaderboards, achievements, and social features. This is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- **Device and diagnostic information:** Standard technical information (device model, OS version, app version) may be collected automatically by Apple and by our advertising partner for compatibility, fraud-prevention, and debugging purposes.

### Information We Do NOT Collect

- We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.
- We do not require account creation or sign-in to play.
- We do not access your location (beyond the coarse, IP-based location our advertising partner may use), camera, photo library, contacts, microphone, or health data.
- We do not operate our own backend server that stores your gameplay data — see Section 2.

---

## 2. Your Game Data: Local Storage and iCloud Sync

Your progress (roster, currencies, campaign progress, settings, and purchase history) is stored **locally on your device**. If you are signed in to iCloud and have iCloud Drive enabled for Dreamkeepers, your save data is additionally synced through **your own personal iCloud account** (Apple's iCloud/CloudKit infrastructure) so that it carries across your devices signed into the same Apple ID.

This sync uses Apple's infrastructure directly — Dreamkeepers does not operate a separate server, and we (the developer) do not have access to your iCloud-stored save data. Apple's handling of this data is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

If you sign out of iCloud or disable iCloud Drive for the app, Dreamkeepers continues to work using only the local copy on your device.

## 3. How We Use Information

The limited technical information described in Section 1 is used to:

- Serve and measure the performance of in-app advertising (rewarded and interstitial ads)
- Provide Game Center leaderboards, achievements, and social features
- Diagnose and fix technical issues
- Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it to build advertising profiles beyond what our advertising partner does on our behalf as described above.

## 6. In-App Purchases and Randomized Items

Dreamkeepers offers optional in-app purchases of virtual currency (Dream Gems, Gold), convenience items (e.g. Arena Tickets, a VIP pass), and two named one-time character unlocks. All purchases are processed through Apple's In-App Purchase system; refund requests must be submitted to Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) — we do not process refunds directly, and we do not see or store your payment details.

Dreamkeepers also includes a randomized item mechanic (the "Summoning Shrine"), through which characters are awarded at random according to published probabilities ("gacha" mechanic). This mechanic:

- Can be engaged using virtual currency earned through gameplay, without spending real money.
- Displays the probability of each rarity tier in-app before you choose to summon.
- Awards only virtual, in-app items with no real-world cash value; nothing awarded through the Summoning Shrine can be exchanged, sold, or redeemed for real money.

Availability of randomized-item purchase mechanics may be restricted or unavailable in certain countries or regions due to local law.

## 9. Your Rights

Because Dreamkeepers does not collect directly-identifying personal information and does not operate a server-side account system, most data subject requests (access, correction, deletion) are best exercised directly:

- **For Game Center and iCloud data:** through your Apple ID / iCloud account settings, or Apple's own privacy tools.
- **For advertising data:** through your device's ATT setting (Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking) and Google's [Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/).
- **For local, on-device save data:** by resetting progress in-app or deleting the app.
